### Step 1: Upload Your Study Materials

Open the "AI Video Creator." You can paste your lecture script, upload a study guide PDF, or import your lesson PPT. Leadde's AI will automatically analyze the text and structure it into video scenes.

### Step 2: Choose Your AI Teacher

Select a professional avatar from our library. Set the tone to "Explanatory" to ensure complex academic topics are delivered clearly. Use the "Shape" tool to highlight key formulas or text on your slides.

### Step 3: Generate and Download

Review your scenes and click "Generate Video." Once your project is complete, you can easily initiate a lecture video download. Save the MP4 file in high definition, ready to be shared with your class.
