## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes — you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate your billing accordingly.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal. Enterprise customers can also arrange invoice billing.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Our Free plan lets you generate 1 video to experience the core features, with no credit card required. If you need more, you can start a 7-day trial of Creator or Professional plans.

What happens if I exceed my video limit?

You can purchase additional video credits or upgrade to a higher plan. We'll notify you before you reach your limit so you can choose the best option.

How do credits translate into videos?

Credit consumption scales with video length and complexity. Simple video uses **~50 credits per minute**, while complex video (Veo / 3D / AI image generation) uses **~100 credits per minute**.

- **Creator** (1,000 credits/mo) — about 20 min of simple video, or 10 min of complex video.
- **Professional** (3,000 credits/mo) — about 60 min of simple video, or 30 min of complex video.
- **Ultra** (9,000 credits/mo) — about 180 min of simple video, or 90 min of complex video.

Exact credit cost is displayed inside the editor before you render, so you always know what a video will consume. Monthly subscription credits don't roll over, but one-time credit packs you buy never expire.
