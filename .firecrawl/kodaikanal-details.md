### **1\. Kurinji Andavar Temple**

Nothing better than beginning a journey by seeking the blessings of Lord Murugan. Kurunji Andavar Temple is one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal. The temple is highly revered by locals. Dedicated to Lord Murugan, you can also seek blessings from Lord Vishnu and Lord Ganesh. Interestingly, the strategic location of the temples offers a serene atmosphere.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Kurinji-Andavar-Temple-1024x675.jpg)_Kurunji Andavar Temple_

You can watch the mesmerising views of the Palani Hill. What’s more? You can meditate, enjoy the tranquillity or click beautiful pictures against idyllic backdrops. The place is generally not crowded, allowing you some alone time in the city. It is best to avoid weekends, as you can find large crowds at [temples in Tamil Nadu](https://www.treebo.com/blog/temples-in-tamil-nadu/) during this time.

**Location:** Kurinji Andavar Temple Road, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

### **2\. Coakers Walk**

Who does not love witnessing the majesty of misty hills? Coakers Walk is one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal with your loved ones. As the name suggests, this is a viewpoint where you can walk and watch the lush green hill. It is definitely a touristy spot, as it offers panoramic views of the small villages. Perched on elevation, the air here is cooler and all you can feel is the freshness!

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Coakers-walk-1024x675.jpg)_Coakers Walk_

What’s more? The walk is surrounded by a few small shops selling unique souvenirs. Watching the soft layers of the mountains sure adds to the beauty of your journey. It is one of the famous tourist attractions in Kodaikanal for photography and romantic strolls. You can easily book [hotels near Coakers Walk Kodaikanal](https://www.treebo.com/hotels-near-coaker's-walk-kodaikanal/) to access this place with much ease.

**Location:** 6FHV+XGC Coakers Walk, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

### **3\. Kodaikanal Lake**

Your vacation remains incomplete without visiting one of the best tourist places in Kodaikanal! Yes, we are talking about the serene Kodaikanal Lake. After all, a little rejuvenation during your holiday is a great deal! Interestingly, this man-made lake is built in a star shape. The water here is fed by the gorgeous Silver Cascade Falls.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Kodaikanal-lake-1024x675.jpg)_Kodaikanal Lake_

Being one of the famous places to visit in Kodaikanal, you have to experience boating here. The lake is generally crowded by many boats as travellers love the tranquillity. The backdrop of crisp mountains adds to the thrill! Adorned by beautiful trees, you can also find many stalls selling [souvenirs from india](https://www.treebo.com/blog/souvenirs-from-india/) and handmade chocolates. So, get ready for a soul-healing destination.

**Location:** Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

### **4\. Bryant Park**

Floral beauty elevates the shine of Bryant Park. That’s exactly why it is one of the top-rated places to visit in Kodaikanal. As foreign as the name sounds, the place totally does justice to that! Imagine watching the sun with the backdrop of a blue sky and verdant landscapes beneath.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Bryant-Park-1024x675.jpg)_Bryant Park_

The park is particularly popular for the floral blooms spread throughout the area. Well-manicured lanes inspire you to take a blissful stroll amidst the greens. You can definitely spot varied species of shrubs, trees and flowers. If photography is what you love, there are multiple spots to pose. It is best to book affordable [hotels near Bryant Park Kodaikanal](https://www.treebo.com/hotels-near-bryant-park-kodaikanal/) to stay close to scenic views.

**Location:** 6FJR+CQ2, Lower Shola Rd, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

### **5\. Guna Cave**

Have you watched the mysterious movie named Manjummel Boys? Because it actually featured a real-life story of Guna Caves. Known to be one of the popular places to visit in Kodaikanal, these caves are home to mystical paths and deep gorges. The place is also known as Devil’s Kitchen, particularly because of the past incidents of people falling into the deep caves.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Guna-Cave-1024x675.jpg)_Guna Caves_

While the dangerous zones remain closed to tourists, you get an amazing opportunity to admire the thick forests. Additionally, you can also see some of the oldest trees here. You can explore the caves, take serene walks or simply admire the beauty. Monkeys are everyday visitors, so beware of them.

**Location:** Pillar Rocks Rd, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

### **6\. Vattakanal Waterfall**

Are you ready for a picturesque adventure during your trip? Vattakanal waterfall is surely one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal for a soothing time. It is a hidden gem residing amidst the deep forests. You need to hike for a few minutes to reach the main falls. Cascading in layers, it surely takes your breath away with its beauty.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Vattakanal-Waterfall-1024x675.jpg)_Vattakanal Waterfall_

Interestingly, it is not heavily commercialised; hence, you can find fewer crowds here. You can get down in the waters or even take a dip. The waterfall is heavy during the monsoon season. It is best to take precautions before stepping into the waters, as the rocks nearby can be slippery. You can easily book affordable [hotels in Vattakanal Kodaikanal](https://www.treebo.com/hotels-in-vattakanal-kodaikanal/), making your journey convenient.

**Location:** 6FCJ+CP3, Vattakanal, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

### **7\. Pine Forest**

The beautiful town is all about natural bliss and healing your soul in different ways. Pine Forest is indeed one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal for an extraordinary experience. Surrounded by different types of pine trees, this forest is ideal for photography and nature lovers. It is more like a peaceful getaway from the hustle of everyday life.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Pine-forest-1024x675.jpg)_Pine Forest_

What makes this place one of the excellent tourist attractions in Kodaikanal is its secluded location. You can spend time alone, read a book, take a romantic walk or admire nature. The place also offers horseback riding if you are interested in a bit of adventure. You can also plan a camping experience by requesting the local authorities.

**Location:** 6F75+FG4, Lake Rd, Berijam, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

### **8\. Chettiar Park**

If you love being surrounded by lush greenery, Chettiar Park is the place to be! It is one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal for a soothing getaway. Chettiar Park is popularly known for its plethora of flowering plants elevating the beauty of the place. You can find different coloured floral beauties all across this place.

### **9\. Kodaikanal Solar Observatory Museum**

Do you wish to watch the sun through a telescope? That does sound dreamy! It is now definitely possible by visiting the Kodaikanal Solar Observatory Museum. Being one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal, it is a great destination to learn about the sun and different aspects of the solar system.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Kodaikanal-Solar-Observatory-Museum-1024x675.jpg)_Kodaikanal Solar Observatory Museum_

You get an excellent opportunity to see the 4th and 5th layers of the sun with the help of experts. The museum also features live solar images, the Fraunhofer spectrum and various telescopes. You can also look at informative displays and browse through books on solar data collection. So, get ready to dive into the world of astrophysics at one of the top-rated tourist attractions in Kodaikanal.

**Location:** 6FJ8+V5C, Observatory Rd, near Govt Rose Garden, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624103

### **10\. Dolphin Nose**

Traversing through the rugged terrain and lush greenery is what makes your journey memorable. That is exactly what you can do at the viewpoint named Dolphin Nose. It is basically a massive rock resembling the shape of a dolphin’s nose. You need to trek downwards to reach this place. Hence, it does require physical fitness and energy.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Dolphin-Nose-1024x675.jpg)_Dolphin Nose_

As you reach this place, you can see gorgeous views of the Palani Hills. You can also witness varied types of pine and eucalyptus trees on your way. Additionally, freshen yourself with snacks, as many fruit juice vendors and food stalls are available here. You can also find pocket-friendly [hotels near Dolphin Nose Kodaikanal](https://www.treebo.com/hotels-near-dolphin-nose-kodaikanal/) for a comfortable stay.

**Location:** 6F7P+Q3W, Vattakanal, Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu 624101

If you love adventure, you can surely explore [trekking destinations in and around Kodaikanal](https://www.treebo.com/blog/trekking-places-in-kodaikanal/) to add a dosage of fun!

### **11\. Wax Museum**

You simply cannot miss visiting the Wax Museum during your vacation. It is one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal for children as well as adults. The museum is renowned for offering an entertaining journey. It takes you through different statues of international artists as well as popular personalities of the country.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Wax-Museum-1024x675.jpg)_Wax Museum_

Some of the famous statues include Adi Shankaracharya, Mahatma Gandhi and Saint Alphonsa. You can see elaborate scenes depicting different religious events. Statues of Lord Krishna and Radha, Sai Baba and Jesus are also worth watching! It also has a cartoon section for little ones. You can conveniently find affordable [hotels near Wax Museum Kodaikanal](https://www.treebo.com/hotels-near-wax-museum-kodaikanal/) for a stay.

### **12\. Silver Cascade Falls**

Even when some places can be far, they are surely worth exploring! Located on the Kodaikanal-Madurai road, Silver Cascade Falls is just that destination. Flowing down from a jaw-dropping 180 feet, it is one of the best places to visit in Kodaikanal. What makes it a wonderful place is its strategic location.

![Check out the best places to visit in Kodaikanal](https://static-blog.treebo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Silver-Cascade-falls-1-1024x675.jpg)_Silver Cascade Falls_

You can easily watch the fall when travelling via the Ghat Road. Witnessing thick forests all around the frothy waters indeed becomes a sight to behold. You can definitely click Instagram-worthy pictures as it offers an eye-catching backdrop. You can also enter the falls and take a quick dip in the calming waters.

**Location:** Silver Cascade Waterfall, Adukkam, Tamil Nadu 624101

## **How to Reach Kodaikanal?**

### **By Air**

Madurai International Airport is the nearest airport to reach Kodaikanal. It is approximately 133 km from the destination. You can easily find flights from the major Indian cities, including Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi. If you wish to rest, booking affordable [hotels in Madurai](https://www.treebo.com/hotels-in-madurai/) is a great idea to freshen yourself before the journey.

### **By Rail**

Kodaikanal has its own railway station, making it convenient to travel here. Trains are frequently available from the major Indian cities.

### **By Road**

Kodaikanal is well connected via road route. You can take the Kodaikanal-Munnar Road (old SH-18) or Bengaluru-Kodaikanal (NH 44 and NH 183). Similarly, you can also find state-run buses from Madurai International Airport and Kodaikanal Railway Station. Lastly, you can always hire a taxi or travel via personal vehicle.

## **Things To Know Before Visiting Kodaikanal**

- Kodaikanal is perched on the hilltop; hence, the temperature can fall at night. It is best to carry warm clothes no matter the season.
- Always negotiate with the taxi drivers before opting for them. It is ideal to understand the rates from a local to be away from high-price scams.
- The monsoon attracts heavy rainfall, so you can visit waterfalls. However, viewpoints may be filled with complete fog.
- Tamil is the local language here. You can learn a few phrases, making it easy to talk.
- Always opt for modest dressing in temples or places of religious significance.
- Make an advance booking for hotels, as Kodaikanal remains crowded during the peak season.
- Try local cuisine to truly enjoy an authentic experience of flavours.
- Due to elevation, you can feel dizzy. It is best to take medicines for altitude sickness. Also, stay hydrated throughout the time.
