Videos can be updated by editing the source content and regenerating. You can also swap avatars, change backgrounds, adjust tile colours, and insert your brand logo without starting from scratch.

Coursebox videos can be embedded or exported to most major LMS platforms and SCORM packages, or hosted directly inside Coursebox if you prefer a white label LMS with native AI course authoring.

Videos are mobile-friendly and accessible on phones, tablets, and desktops, including through branded mobile apps.
