## How to create a course with AI

Start of list: How to create a course with AI

[Skip to end of “How to create a course with AI” list](https://www.canva.com/features/ai-course-creator/#_r_a_)

### Launch Canva

Open Canva on your mobile app or desktop browser to start building a course with our free AI course generator.

### Open the AI course creator

Access Magic Activities by clicking **Canva AI** above the search bar and selecting **Create student activities**.

### Enter a text prompt

Write a prompt or upload your files for a more accurate activity suggestion. Choose the grade and subject it’s for, as well as the learning purpose, then click the arrow button.

### Generate the course

Define your learning objectives, then select which among the provided activities you’d like to generate. Click **Generate activities** and watch our AI-assisted course creator build the lessons.

### Refine the course

Customize the lessons to match your teaching style and creative vision. Swap out fonts, color palettes, and elements. Preview the designs as a student would by clicking **View as learner**.

### Assign the course

Once you’re done, click **Assign course** and enter the names of the learners you want to assign it to. Select the toggle if you want to notify your learners of the assignment, then hit **Assign**.

## Frequently asked questions

- * * *

- What is an AI course creator?


An AI course creator is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to help you create and design educational content faster. It can generate lesson outlines, slides, quizzes, and more based on the topic or input you provide, saving you time while giving you a solid starting point.

- * * *

- How accurate are AI-generated course materials?


AI-generated content is built on widely available knowledge and best practices. While it provides a good starting point, we strongly recommend reviewing and customizing materials to match your goals, teaching style, and class needs.

- * * *

- How long does it take to create a course on Canva?


You can create an online course in just seconds. From outline to slides, our AI online course creator gets to work instantly, turning your idea into a course that’s ready to edit and share. Just type in your prompt, select the subject and grade, define the learning objective, and wait for the AI to do its job.

- * * *

- Is there a free AI course creator available?


Our AI course creator is free for Canva for Education users. You will need to get verified before having access to the best AI course creator, plus a bunch of other classroom-ready tools at no extra cost. Check if you’re eligible [here](https://www.canva.com/education/eligibility-guidelines/).
