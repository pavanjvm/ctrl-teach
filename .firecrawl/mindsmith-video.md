## The AI agent understands your video

Upload a video and Mindsmith's AI agent watches, transcribes, and indexes the whole thing. It can find specific clips, take screenshots at any timestamp, extract quotes, identify key moments, and break footage into scenes. Then tell it what to create. Turn a recorded meeting into a structured course. Convert a product demo into an interactive walkthrough. Transform compliance footage into scenario-based training.

![AI agent breaking up a video into lesson pages](https://www.mindsmith.ai/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fscreenshot-video-ai.png&w=3840&q=75&dpl=dpl_5pRfSubnQBmWmVu3vraCDS1PmxoS)

### Full courses

One video becomes a complete video-based eLearning course. Longer videos get split into chapters, each becoming its own section. Text summaries, visual elements, and knowledge checks generated automatically.

### Branching Scenario

Turn a recorded role play or customer interaction into an interactive branching experience.

### Software Simulation

Upload a screen recording and the AI recreates it as an interactive walkthrough.

### Key Moments

The agent identifies the most important segments and builds a highlights reel with quiz questions.

## Auto-generated transcripts and captions

Upload a video and Mindsmith generates a full transcript automatically. Transcripts convert to captions that learners toggle on or off. Captions translate with the lesson.

- Auto-transcription from any video source
- WebVTT captions with smart sentence breaking
- Captions translate with the lesson
- Full-text search across video transcripts
