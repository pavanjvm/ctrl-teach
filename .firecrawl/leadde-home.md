### What is Leadde?

Leadde is dedicated to the intelligent generation of multilingual training videos. With Leadde, you can quickly transform static documents into engaging, dynamic instructional videos. It is perfect for enhancing engagement in training, marketing, or internal communications.

### Can I create a video with only a voice-over?

Yes, you can. Simply delete or hide the Avatar layer to create a video with voice-over only.

### How to save my video edits?

If you are properly connected to the internet, your video edits will be automatically saved.
