## Frequently Asked Questions

[What pricing plans does Coursebox offer?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox offers a range of pricing plans including a free tier for creating 3 courses and paid plans for creators, white label LMS deployments, businesses and enterprise teams to access more advanced features and more AI credits.

[Is there a free version of Coursebox available?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox includes a free plan that allows users to start building courses and exploring the core AI features at no cost, with unlimited learners and no credit card required to begin.

[Are paid plans required to create courses with Coursebox?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Paid plans are not required to create courses, but upgrading to a paid tier unlocks higher usage limits, advanced AI credits, branded LMS options, more features and expanded support for larger teams and more content. You can create 3 courses for free without giving your credit card details.

[What features are included in Coursebox’s paid plans?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Paid Coursebox plans include increased AI usage credits, more generated courses, AI videos, branded courses and certificates, private publishing, SCORM and LTI export, and additional AI tools such as assessment and chatbot support.

[Can I scale my Coursebox plan as my needs grow?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox plans can scale as your training needs grow, allowing you to move from free usage to creator and branded LMS tiers with more AI capabilities and higher content limits.

[Does Coursebox offer an enterprise plan?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox offers enterprise pricing and custom plans for larger organisations that need advanced branding, custom domains, multiple course admins, and significant AI usage allowances.

[Are there limits on learners with Coursebox plans?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox allows unlimited learners on all plans, including the free plan, allowing you to train small teams or large organisations without seat-based fees.

[How does Coursebox price AI usage?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox plans include a set number of AI credits or generated text, images, and videos per month or year, with higher tiers providing larger allowances for heavier usage.

[Can Coursebox be used by individuals and small teams?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox can be used by individuals and small teams, as the free and creator plans offer accessible features that allow course creation and basic AI tools without enterprise commitments.

[Does Coursebox offer discounts for annual billing?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox pricing generally displays both monthly and yearly options, with the yearly plans discounted compared to month-to-month billing.

[Can I try Coursebox before committing?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Yes, Coursebox allows you to start with the free tier so you can explore the platform and its features before upgrading to a paid plan for higher usage or advanced capabilities.

[Can I sell courses created in Coursebox?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox allows you to sell courses and training content, with plans supporting ecommerce and monetisation features including recurring subscriptions.

[Are there additional add-ons or custom options for Coursebox pricing?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox includes add-on and custom pricing options for enterprise needs such as larger AI credit bundles, addtional AI Video credits and other custom solutions.

[Is pricing based on features or users?](https://www.coursebox.ai/pricing#)

Coursebox pricing is primarily based on feature tiers and usage allowances rather than user count, with unlimited learners supported across all plans.
