# Simple, Transparent Pricing That Grows With You

All prices are per author. Educators get 30% off, and learners are always free.

MonthlyAnnualSave 20%

### Free

$0/month

billed annually at $0/year

Get started with the basics

- 450 AI credits at signup (one-time)
- Up to 10 team members
- Unlimited content and sharing
- Includes "Built with Mindsmith" badge
- Link, embed, and SCORM sharing
- Create and edit lessons
- Basic chat and email support

[Start Free](https://app.mindsmith.ai/signup)

### Professional

$39/author/month

billed annually at $468/year

For individual creators and small teams

- 2,000 AI credits per author / month
- Up to 10 seats
- Removes "Built with Mindsmith" badge
- Audio narration with text-to-speech
- Custom branding and theming
- Add your logo
- Generate scenarios
- View engagement and performance metrics

[Get Started](https://app.mindsmith.ai/signup)

### Business

$65/author/month

billed annually at $780/year

For teams and collaboration

- 5,000 AI credits per author / month
- Add additional credits as needed
- Up to 10 seats
- Live collaboration
- Version history and staging
- Export engagement metrics as PDF
- ElevenLabs integration
- Priority support

[Get Started](https://app.mindsmith.ai/signup)
