## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need technical skills to use Coursebox?

Coursebox uses an interactive chat editor and requires no coding. If you can use a Google Doc, you can build a course in Coursebox.

### Can Coursebox integrate with my existing LMS or HR system?

Coursebox exports to SCORM and LTI, plugging into most existing LMS platforms. It also integrates with Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace and hundreds of other tools via Zapier. Additionally, it can also be used as a standalone LMS with your branding.

### Is there a limit on learners?

All plans, including the free tier, support unlimited active learner enrolments.

### Can I sell courses through Coursebox?

Coursebox supports paid course delivery on all plans, with direct integrations for Stripe, PayPal, and Shopify. You can also integrate with your favourite tools through Zapier.

### What is an AI course creator?

An AI course creator turns a prompt or document into a complete, structured online course including lessons, quizzes, videos and assessments in minutes.

### Can I edit and customise the courses it creates?

Coursebox AI generates a draft course from your prompt or documents. This draft course sits within the easy to use course editor. Course content can be edited manually or edited by chatting with AI. Additional content can be added and the course editor is very flexible.

### How is Coursebox different from using ChatGPT?

Structure, delivery, hosting, branding, integrations. Coursebox does many things a generic LLMs and chatbots can't do.
